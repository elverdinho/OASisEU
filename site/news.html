Sinergies in Action! OASis became partner of the Partecipatory Planning Master implemented by IUAV University of Venice

29/10/2025

OASis is proud to join the IUAV University of Venice, Fondazione Campania Welfare, and the Spazio Aggregativo di Prossimità (SAP) in the Master “Verso un Hub di Comunità”, a university training program on co-design and community regeneration taking place at Parco San Laise (former NATO base, Bagnoli, Naples) from September 2025 to January 2026.

The master engages local associations, community groups, and cultural actors in participatory processes aimed at strengthening networks and fostering civic innovation.

Members of the OASis team, together with artists and outcomes from the project’s co-design residencies, will enrich the program by sharing concrete examples of how participatory and collaborative methodologies can be applied to the cultural and creative sectors.
