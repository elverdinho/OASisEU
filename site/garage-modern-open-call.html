Le Garage Moderne - France - Deadline 01/04 and 07/04/2025

Le Garage Moderne is launching two artist residency programs in framework of OASis program:

Residency #1 Take part in the transformation of Le Garage Moderne & co-create local responses to the current global challenges
Dates of residency: May 3 - 17

Get involved, with your art, in a community project involving a Banksy exhibition + 50 civic and cultural organisations in Bordeaux and take part in the co-creation of the a space dedicated to open art residencies at Le Garage Moderne

Related topics: Migrations - Climate activism - Cultural and Human rights.

Deadline: April 1 - 11:59 AM

Residency #2 Recreate Le Garage Moderne in Bordeaux Contemporary Art Center

Dates of residency: June 1 - 15

Build with us a pop-up Garage Moderne : a re-creation of ourselves, complete with a pop-up kitchen, a pop-up Bicycle workshop and a concerts and conference program, in Bordeaux Contemporary Art Museum

Related topics : Arts and craft, right to repair, grassroot culture, community organising

Deadline: April 7 - 11:59 AM

What to include in your application:

1. Statement of intention – Share your ideas & how you’d collaborate with locals and/or artists!

2. Portfolio or website – Show us your work!

3. Two photos – You & You at your (art) work

Conditions:

  • Accommodation and catering included

  • Travel budget up to 290 €

  • Fees of 1300 € gross (including taxes)

  • Limited budget for production/material expenses.

How to apply:
By sending e-mail to programmation@legaragemoderne.org with the subject “OASis - residency nr 1” or “OASis - residency nr 2” (note: the size of the email with attachments can not exceeds 10 MB)
